Independent makers

Rent a Stall at Briarbridge Market

Approved independent makers will be able to operate their own Briarbridge stalls. Rental price, availability, commission structure, payout schedule, and vendor terms remain TO CONFIRM until you set them.

Planned vendor protection: each approved maker gets a separate stall profile and protected vendor area. Vendors will not receive access to another maker's orders, customer records, payment information, or private administration.

No application data is transmitted from this preview. The production form will use authenticated, server-side storage rather than exposing submissions in the browser.

Protected commerce

Briarbridge Basket & Secure Checkout

The storefront can stay completely custom while payment processing happens through a PCI-compliant payment service behind the scenes.

What the live shop will protect

  • Raw card numbers are never stored in Tree & Ember code or databases.
  • Orders and totals are validated on the server before checkout.
  • Admin and vendor areas require protected authentication and authorization.
  • Digital purchases use controlled delivery rather than public file links.
  • HTTPS is required for production.
  • Vendor stalls are separated so makers only see records they are allowed to access.
